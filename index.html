About the Project

Turco Falando AutoPost is a personal content creation project focused on Brazilian Portuguese content about Turkish language, culture, traditions and everyday life.

The project uses local computer-based tools to assist with content planning, script generation, video creation, voice generation and editing.

How It Works

Content is created locally on the user's computer. Videos are prepared for short-form social media publishing and may be submitted to TikTok through authorized publishing functionality.

Purpose

The purpose of this project is to simplify the workflow involved in creating educational and cultural short videos while keeping the production process transparent and user-controlled.
