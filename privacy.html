1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Turco Falando AutoPost handles information in connection with its website and content automation workflow.

The project is designed with a local-first approach and aims to minimize the collection and processing of personal information.

2. Information We Collect

The website does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through forms, accounts or registration systems.

When a user contacts the project, information voluntarily provided by the user may be received for the purpose of responding to the request.

3. Local Processing

The content creation workflow may process scripts, text, images, audio and video files locally on the user's computer.

Local files used during content creation are not automatically uploaded to this website.

4. Third-Party Services

The project may interact with third-party services and platforms when the authorized user chooses to use their functionality.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms of service.

In particular, TikTok may receive information and content when a user authorizes publishing through an official TikTok integration.

5. TikTok Authorization

If the project is authorized to interact with a TikTok account, the authorization is provided by the account owner.

Turco Falando AutoPost does not claim ownership of the user's TikTok account or its content.

Users may manage or revoke third-party authorization according to the options provided by the relevant platform.

6. Cookies and Tracking

This website does not intentionally use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or behavioral advertising technologies.

GitHub Pages or other infrastructure providers may process technical information necessary to deliver and secure the website according to their own policies.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to limit unnecessary access to information handled by the project.

However, no internet transmission or digital storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Information voluntarily provided through contact requests may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request or maintain the relevant communication.

9. Children's Privacy

This project is not specifically directed toward children. The project does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to the project, applicable requirements or third-party integrations.

Updated versions will be published on this page.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the contact information provided on the Contact page.
