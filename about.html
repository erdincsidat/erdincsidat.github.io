What is Turco Falando AutoPost?

Turco Falando AutoPost is a personal content creation project designed to simplify the process of producing short-form educational and cultural videos for social media.

The project focuses primarily on content related to Turkish language, Turkish culture, traditions, food, travel and everyday life, presented for Brazilian Portuguese-speaking audiences.

The goal is to make cultural information accessible, entertaining and easy to understand through short-form video.

Why Was It Created?

Creating short-form video content involves several repetitive steps, including researching ideas, writing scripts, preparing visual materials, generating narration, editing videos and preparing content for publication.

Turco Falando AutoPost was created to organize these steps into a simpler and more efficient workflow while keeping the content creator in control of the final result.

Our approach: technology should assist the creator, not replace the creator's responsibility for the final content.

Local-First Approach

One of the main principles of the project is a local-first approach.

Whenever practical, content preparation can be performed using software and tools installed on the creator's own computer. This may include script generation, media preparation, narration and video editing.

This approach helps reduce unnecessary dependence on external services during the content creation process.

Content Creation Workflow

The project can assist with several stages of the creative workflow:

  • Finding and organizing content ideas
  • Preparing short-form video scripts
  • Creating or organizing visual materials
  • Preparing voice narration
  • Combining audio and video materials
  • Preparing videos for social media publication
  • Supporting authorized social media publishing workflows

The exact workflow may change as the project develops.

Human Review

Generated or automatically prepared content may contain errors, outdated information or material that requires additional review.

For this reason, content should be reviewed by the creator before publication.

The creator remains responsible for the final video, description, hashtags, privacy settings and publication decisions.

Social Media Integration

Turco Falando AutoPost may use official APIs and authorized integrations provided by social media platforms.

When a platform requires account authorization, the account owner remains in control of that authorization and the associated account.

The project does not claim ownership of third-party social media accounts or their content.

Our Goal

The long-term goal of Turco Falando AutoPost is to create a practical, transparent and creator-controlled workflow for producing useful short-form educational and cultural content.

At its heart, the project is about sharing Turkish culture with Brazilian audiences while making the creative process more efficient through technology.

Transparency

We aim to keep the project transparent about how content is created, how third-party services may be used and where human review remains important.
